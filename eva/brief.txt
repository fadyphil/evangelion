PRODUCT TRUTH
Evangelion is a Bible reading + comprehension app for users aged 12–18. A teen reads a short Bible passage (English or Arabic), then answers a 1–5 question multiple-choice quiz with instant feedback. Tone targets: warm, clever, energetic, trustworthy — never childish, never churchy, never corporate. The scripture experience stays calm and beautiful; the surrounding app (home, progress, results) carries the color and playfulness.

THE CONCEPT — "THE ILLUMINATED STICKERBOOK"
A warm paper-diary world where scripture is set like a beautiful printed book, and everything around it is decorated with bright, flat, sticker-like color accents. Think: a teen's beautifully kept journal that happens to contain the most elegantly typeset text they've ever read. Modern editorial layout + playful sticker accents. NOT medieval, NOT a cathedral, NOT a classroom.

Elevation: FLAT design. Cards sit on canvas via 1px hairline border OR an extremely soft ambient shadow (0 8 24 rgba(34,28,21,0.06)). Never hard drop shadows. No glassmorphism, no blur effects.

GLOBAL BANS (violating any of these = regenerate the screen):

- NO cards nested inside cards.
- NO rounded-square icon tile sitting above a heading as a section header.
- NO bounce easing; motion uses gentle ease-out, 200–300ms.
- NO Inter, NO Roboto, NO system font.
- NO drop shadows on buttons. NO 3D, NO skeuomorphism, NO glass.
- NO crosses, doves, stained glass, halos, or any religious iconography. The ONLY sacred cues are: gold flecks (tiny, sparse, decorative), the verse marker, and beautiful typography.
- NO Duolingo mascots, confetti explosions, or XP bars. Playfulness comes from color, type, and microcopy only.
